Method, briefly Pace's own currently-published GTFS schedule was checked stop-by-stop against real vehicle GPS pings collected directly from Pace's public live-tracking feed, once every ~60 seconds, 24/7, over three complete calendar days (Aug 30–Sep 1, 2026). A scheduled stop is counted as missed only if no vehicle was recorded within 500 meters and 15 minutes of that stop's real scheduled time, generous enough to allow for normal traffic and light schedule slack.
