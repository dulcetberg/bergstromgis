Most underserved

    Right now, globally

    Requirements and funding: UN OCHA Financial Tracking Service, via the public Humanitarian Programme Cycle API, live 2026 data. Population, people in need and people targeted come from the same OCHA API, from each response plan's own published caseload figures. Country boundaries: Natural Earth. 31 countries with an active 2026 response plan; a further $1.34 billion in real funding went to regional or global appeals not tied to one country, and isn't shown on this map.

    Five entries here are refugee response plans (Lebanon, Bangladesh, Iran, Djibouti, Uganda). Those report a refugee caseload rather than a host country population, so they have no total population figure and are shown in grey under that option. People in need is the one figure published for every country in the dataset.

    Every population figure can be replaced with your own: click a country, type over the number, and the map, the ranking and the per person maths all update immediately. Your figures are saved in this browser and can be reverted to OCHA's at any time.
    Click a country to see its real 2026 humanitarian requirement, how much has actually been funded, how many people it covers, and by whom it's paid for.
